# WRECK — scroll-craft BRIEF

Source: user-directed, with explicit creative delegation on the visual + motion layer.
Content, copy and IA are preserved from the existing site.

## 1. Vibe (3–5 words) + references
Bright industrial discipline. High-contrast, athletic, engineered. Not moody-dark:
the recent design direction is a bright sand palette with a single molten accent.
References (medium, not sites): a well-lit modern strength gym at midday; the
matte-and-machined feel of premium training hardware; editorial sports photography.

## 2. Scroll journey (their content, section by section)
Hero (your fitness, built around you) → the problem (fitness is fragmented) →
the turn (one adaptive model of you) → the systems (training, nutrition,
adaptation, coach, progress) → the eight training modes → build my WRECK.

## 3. Energy curve
Calm-charged open, sharp friction at the problem, a bright release at the turn,
weighty and deliberate through the systems, wide and open at the modes, firm
close.

## 4. Feeling curve (one line per act) + peak
1 Hero — charged anticipation: you, lit, still, about to move.
2 Fragmented — friction: scattered apps, none of them know you.
3 The Reforge — release/clarity: the fragments weld into one plan.  ← PEAK
4 The systems — gravity/respect: the machinery under the calm.
5 Eight modes — possibility: it bends to whoever you are.
6 Build — resolve: one action, taken.

Peak: Act 3. It gets the most scroll room and the quiet beat before it.

## 5. One thing no site does — signature move
"The Reforge": scrolling Act 3, scattered fragments (a workout, a meal, a
check-in) physically converge and weld into one dashboard, a Vulcanico weld-line
igniting along the seam as they fuse. Bespoke, coded in-page (wreck → rebuild).

## 6. Distance from premium-minimal
Bold editorial, not minimal, not maximalist. Big American Captain display,
hard cuts, one hot accent, generous negative space on sand.

## 7. One unbroken world or distinct scenes
Distinct scenes with hard cuts. Grammar: chaptered cuts (not filmic one-shot,
not worldflight).

## 8. Assets on hand
Fonts (American Captain, Gloucester MT), tokens (Vulcanico #FF4103, Noturno
#001621, sand #F4F2EC), the real product UI (ModeDemo). Old stock photos and AI
machine renders are being removed and replaced with freshly generated stills.

## Tell-someone sentence
It's the site where the scattered pieces of your training visibly weld into one
plan as you scroll.

## Authored silence
A held quiet beat right before Act 3 (the Reforge) — intentional, not dead scroll.
